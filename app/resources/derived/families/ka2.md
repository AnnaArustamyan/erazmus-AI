# KA2 partnerships — KA210 (small-scale) and KA220 (cooperation)

Thin pack for partnerships. Do **not** apply KA153 youth-worker mobility rules or rewrite this as a training course for youth workers.

Award-criteria shape: relevance, quality of partnership, design and work packages, impact, management.

## Always

1. This is a **partnership**, not a KA1 mobility. Activities are work packages, meetings, local delivery, and shared results — not “12 youth workers on a five-day course” unless that is one WP inside a real consortium.
2. Needs evidence at organisation and target-group level, with method. Priorities (inclusion, digital, green, participation) only where activities actually deliver them.
3. Partnership: organisations in at least two Programme countries (application stage), complementary roles, why these partners, who coordinates.
4. KA210: proportionate to a small partnership and the chosen lump sum (EUR 30 000 or 60 000). Newcomers are expected; do not fake a KA220-scale work plan.
5. KA220: work packages with lead partner, tasks, timing, and results others can reuse. Budget logic matches the work.
6. Impact at the real scale of the partnership, with indicators, dissemination channels, and what continues after EU funding (this project’s results).
7. Management: coordination, quality, risk, monitoring of work packages.

## Never

1. Never apply “the right beneficiary is youth workers” or KA153 selection-of-youth-workers rules to KA210/KA220.
2. Never invent partners in a second country.
3. Never write a mobility timetable as the whole application if the action is KA2.
4. Never claim field-level systemic impact from a EUR 30 000 small-scale project.

## Failure notes (synthetic, Guide-faithful)

- **KA2-FAIL-1 — Solo applicant.** One organisation, no partner in another Programme country, described as if it were staff mobility abroad.
- **KA2-FAIL-2 — Priority as slogan.** “Digital transformation” in the tick-box with no digital activity or result.
- **KA2-FAIL-3 — Work packages missing.** A KA220 draft lists workshops but no WP leads, no shared output, and no management of the consortium.

# KA1 education mobility — KA121 / KA122 / KA131-171

Thin pack for school, VET, adult-education, and higher-education mobility. Do **not** apply KA153 youth-worker rules.

Award-criteria shape: objectives and needs, participants, activities and learning outcomes, recognition and follow-up, management (plus accreditation check for KA121).

## Always

1. Name the education field (school / VET / adult / higher education) and the occupational or subject need — not only a theme such as “green” or “digital”.
2. Needs analysis must show a method, when it was done, who was asked, and what they said. Staff needs and learner needs are different.
3. Objectives are SMART and chained to those needs. The same list appears in activities, learning outcomes, and impact.
4. Participants: role (staff / learners / students), numbers, profile, published selection criteria. Inclusion: named barriers and support, not a slogan.
5. Activities say what people will physically do (course, job-shadowing, placement, teaching assignment) with duration and host country.
6. Learning outcomes are knowledge / skills / attitudes used after return. Recognition is planned (certificate, Europass, ECTS, learning agreement, internal validation).
7. Label **KA121 only** if the organisation holds a valid Erasmus accreditation in that field. Otherwise this is usually KA122 (or the accredited call is the wrong product).
8. KA131/171: inter-institutional agreements, ECTS or equivalent recognition, and sending/receiving roles.

## Never

1. Never inject KA153 “right beneficiary is youth workers” into a school, VET, adult, or HEI mobility.
2. Never invent accreditation, partner names, or ECTS numbers.
3. Never treat a slogan (“innovative”, “greener earth”) as an objective.
4. Never write a KA2 work-package partnership as if it were this mobility.

## Failure notes (synthetic, Guide-faithful)

- **EDU-FAIL-1 — No needs method.** “We want to internationalise” with no survey, focus group, or quality-plan evidence. Reviewers cannot score Relevance.
- **EDU-FAIL-2 — KA121 without accreditation.** The draft calls the action KA121 but the organisation has no Erasmus accreditation. That is the wrong form; use KA122 or obtain accreditation first.
- **EDU-FAIL-3 — Outcomes without recognition.** Participants “will learn a lot” with no certificate, ECTS, Europass, or workplace follow-up. Learning evaporates after the trip.

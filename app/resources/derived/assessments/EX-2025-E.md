## Strengths

- Coordinator in youth work; theme can touch transversal competences for workers
- All partners described as newcomers
- Mobility programme generally well structured; appropriate non-formal methods
- Numeric and gender balance (equal participants per partner)
- Selection includes qualitative criteria and transparent steps
- Project phases match Guide expectations; digital tools; safety/insurance
- Inclusion and active participation mentioned (but see weaknesses)

## Weaknesses (reviewer)

- Purpose not clear or consistent; **different objectives in different sections**
- Relevance arguments generic; weakly tied to partners’ actual work
- Major inconsistencies (participant numbers, types of analysis, partner count, objectives)
- Needs–objectives–activities–results–impact chain missing
- Learning outcomes formal, not linked to the training programme
- Impact overstated vs project scale
- APV detailed programme not annexed → activity not recommended for funding
- Preparation and follow-up thin; conflicting participant numbers
- Participant profile differs by section; selection criteria without weightings
- Unclear that each partner can actually select the stated number of workers
- Inclusion vague; barriers of participants with fewer opportunities not identified
- Management only partly described; partnership composition criteria unclear
- Result indicators inconsistent; no evaluation of prep/follow-up

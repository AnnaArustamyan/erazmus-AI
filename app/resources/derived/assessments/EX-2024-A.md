## Strengths (reviewer)

- Indicators and targets
- Newcomer organisations involved
- Intention to use green practices and virtual communication

## Weaknesses (reviewer)

- Needs analysis: social-media trends for young people are described, but it is unclear how needs of **youth workers** and of **each participating organisation** were established; “workshops, seminars and surveys” not evidenced
- Some learning results (video production, project management, graphic design) less relevant to youth-work practice
- Participant profile unclear: not specified that participants are consortium staff/volunteers; selection described as open call from communities
- Objectives not SMART
- Activity methodology thin; programme emphasises blogging/content production rather than a needs → activities → professional competences chain
- Contribution to **organisational capacity** (core KA153 aim) only partial if participants are not staff of the partners

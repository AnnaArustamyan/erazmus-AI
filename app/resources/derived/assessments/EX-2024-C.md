- Year: 2024
- Total: **45 / 100** (Relevance 12 / Design 18 / Management 15)
- Paired example: `applications/EX-2024-C.pdf` (local PDF, do not commit)

## Strengths

- Newcomers
- Green / virtual elements intended
- Evaluation section relatively clear (what will be evaluated, tools, indicators)

## Weaknesses (reviewer)

- Unclear which **horizontal priority** the project addresses
- Activities and learning results centred on **personal development / mental wellbeing**, not professional development of youth workers → weak Action fit
- Needs analysis: activities of one partner described; unclear if other countries’ workers were consulted; “10 studies” named without sources
- Main identified need is burnout; proposed solution is wellness activities rather than youth-work competences
- Learning outcomes not structured as knowledge / skills / attitudes; several results not relevant to work with young people (e.g. “reduce dopamine dependence”)

# EX-2024-B — Democracy + tech entrepreneurship TC

- Action: Mobility of youth workers
- Year: 2024
- Total: **41 / 100** (Relevance 10 / Design 18 / Management 13)
- Paired example: `applications/EX-2024-B.pdf` (local PDF, do not commit)

## Strengths

- Newcomer organisations
- Green practices and virtual communication intended

## Weaknesses (reviewer)

- Participants not clearly consortium staff → weak contribution to organisational capacity; Action objectives only partly addressed
- Needs analysis weak: references concentrated on one country / two organisations; centred on **young entrepreneurs**, not youth workers
- Logic break: needs (startup ideas, business plans, DemTech ventures) do not match what a youth-worker training course can deliver; indicators such as “% of teams present a startup idea / develop a business plan” are Youth-Exchange / incubator logic, not KA153
- Learning results include items irrelevant to youth-work jobs
- Venue poorly justified (activity in a partner country without a clear operational reason)
- Dissemination: many measures, but no timeline, unclear target groups, missing indicators
